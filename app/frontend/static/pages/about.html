About PUFShield

Building device trust from hardware upward.

PUFShield is a hardware-rooted security platform designed to establish verifiable trust in devices from their physical characteristics through the complete boot process.

Our Mission

Three principles guide every layer of the PUFShield platform.

Hardware Root of Trust
Establish device identity from unique physical characteristics that cannot be cloned or transferred.
Defense in Depth
Never rely on a single security layer. Combine physical, cryptographic, behavioral, and transparency-based verification.
Quantum Resilience
Prepare device security for the post-quantum era with lattice-based cryptography and zero-knowledge proofs.

Why It Matters

Modern connected devices — from IoT sensors to critical infrastructure — require stronger mechanisms for identity, firmware integrity, and trusted execution.

Device Identity
Every device needs a unique, unclonable identity rooted in hardware.
Firmware Integrity
Software integrity must be verifiable from silicon to application.
Secure Boot
Trust must be established and verified before any code executes.

Physical Unclonability
SRAM PUF leverages inherent manufacturing variations in silicon to create device fingerprints that are physically impossible to replicate, even by the manufacturer.
Cryptographic Proofs
ECDSA signatures, ML-DSA-65 post-quantum signatures, and zero-knowledge proofs provide mathematical guarantees of authenticity without exposing secrets.
Behavioral Monitoring
AI-powered anomaly detection continuously monitors device behavior, flagging abnormal patterns in environmental conditions, timing, and operational metrics.
Transparency Verification
Merkle tree transparency logs ensure every firmware publication is recorded and auditable, preventing supply-chain attacks through hidden or unauthorized releases.
