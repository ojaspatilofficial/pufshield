Modern Devices Need More Than a Digital Password

Connected devices face evolving threats that traditional security approaches cannot fully address.

Cloned Device Identity
Attackers attempt to reproduce device credentials to impersonate trusted hardware.
Firmware Tampering
Malicious firmware modifications can compromise an otherwise trusted device.
Quantum Threat
Future quantum computers may break traditional public-key cryptography.
Physical Tampering
Hardware manipulation creates abnormal physical behavior that must be detected.
Stolen Signing Keys
A compromised manufacturer key can be used to sign unauthorized firmware.
Trust Without Transparency
A valid signature alone does not prove firmware was officially published.

What is PUFShield?

PUFShield is a hardware-rooted security platform that combines SRAM PUF technology with cryptographic identity, post-quantum cryptography, and zero-knowledge proofs to create a multi-layered device authentication and secure boot system.

By anchoring trust in the physical properties of silicon rather than stored secrets, PUFShield eliminates single points of failure. Each device generates a unique identity from manufacturing variations that cannot be cloned, extracted, or transferred.

The platform layers AI anomaly detection, Merkle transparency logs, and a composite risk engine on top of the hardware root of trust, delivering end-to-end firmware integrity from silicon to boot.
